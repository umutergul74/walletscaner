# Scoring

Scores are deterministic and explainable. A token receives a 0-100 score with sub-scores:

- freshness
- liquidity quality
- volume quality
- holder distribution
- smart-wallet participation
- creator reputation
- contract/token safety
- market structure
- social/metadata credibility
- risk penalties

Risk penalties are applied for authority issues, holder concentration, suspected wash trading, bot-only flow, insider concentration, poor liquidity depth, and rapid liquidity removal.

Wallets are scored separately. A high early-entry count is not enough; the wallet must show repeatability, exits, rug avoidance, and copyability after latency and slippage.
